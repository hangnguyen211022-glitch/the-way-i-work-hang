The Way I Work

Hang

Instructional Designer · Learning Strategist · Curious Mind

✨ I work brilliantly together with…
  • People who start with why before jumping to how
  • Collaborators willing to question whether something should exist before building it — driven by genuine curiosity, not just compliance
  • People who can hold space for reflection and aren't put off by "but what's the real problem we're solving?"
💡 You'll get me on board by…
  • Showing me the learner at the centre of the decision
  • Bringing evidence, sharing real context, and trusting me with the messy details
  • Leading with honesty
  • Knowing that reasoning matters more to me
🔥 What motivates me each day…
  • The moment something clicks
  • Work that is genuinely useful, not just technically complete
  • Understanding how people learn, grow, and make sense of their experiences
  • Intellectual curiosity - it's my constant engine
⛈️ What gets in my way…
  • Content that exists for the sake of existing
  • Jargon used without meaning or explanation
  • Environments where feedback is withheld and I have to guess where I stand
  • Spaces where foundational questions are seen as a weakness
🌊 When I'm under stress…
  • I process by articulating - talking or writing through the problem helps me find clarity
  • I may go quiet first to make sense of things internally before I'm ready to engage
  • I protect my energy carefully and recover best with space
💬 Things I'd love to resolve as a team…
  • How we know when learning content is actually working — not just finished
  • How we protect space for learner-centred thinking even when timelines are tight
  • How we build a culture that is constructive, transparent, and respectful
🏆 My top three strengths

  • surface insights

My value isn't just in finding answers, but in asking better questions.

🌟 What I need to do my best work…
Purpose-driven work I'm most engaged when I can see how my contribution connects to a meaningful outcome, not just a deliverable
Proper information access Being kept in the loop allows me to prepare thoroughly and show up with confidence
Visibility Knowing my work is seen and valued by the people who matter helps me stay motivated and grow
Respectful collaboration I contribute best in environments where different perspectives are welcomed and people feel safe to speak honestly
Stretch opportunities Being trusted with meaningful, complex work signals that my growth is taken seriously
Consistent touchpoints Regular check-ins give me the clarity and direction I need to prioritise well and stay aligned
💌 The best way to give me feedback…
  • Be constructive and specific — I want to know where I can grow
  • Tell me why something landed a certain way, not just what
  • Deliver it in conversation rather than leaving it ambiguous
  • "Here's what I noticed" - direct observation lands better than leaving me to interpret the silence
